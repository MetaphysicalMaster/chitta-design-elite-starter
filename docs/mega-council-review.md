# METAMARKER.COM — MEGA COUNCIL REVIEW & EXECUTION DOSSIER

**Engagement:** Landing-page strategic teardown + rebuild
**Mandate:** Deliver the clarity, conviction, and conversion architecture a $1,000,000 review would buy.
**Operating thesis (non-negotiable, set by the principal):**

1. **Leave "what is metamarketing" a mystery.** The page never defines it. The mystery IS the hook. Visitors who don't get it are free to bounce — we want that filter.
2. **Sell the outcome, not the philosophy.** The old page was heavy on *why metaphysical marketing matters as a concept*. Kill all of it. We talk about **results**.
3. **Results are exponential, not additive.** This is the spine of the whole page.
4. **Attribution is unambiguous.** Visitors must understand: *you will see the exact day your business transforms, and exactly why.* No "marketing is fuzzy."
5. **We build web apps, not just website redesigns.** Showcase this dynamically — ideally by making the page itself behave like the product.

> **Council verdict in one line:** The previous page sold a *toolchain* to *developers*. Metamarker sells an *inflection point* to *business owners*. Every element was off-target. We rebuilt from the studs.

---

## 0. How the Council convened

Three tiers of review, exactly as commissioned:

- **Tier 1 — Individual board members** file personal reviews (their lens only).
- **Tier 2 — Segment guilds** (Distro, Design Gods, Growth/Board, Engineering) converge each tier-1 set into a guild consensus.
- **Tier 3 — C-Suite** (CEO, CMO, CDO, CTO, CFO, CXO) rule on the guild consensuses.
- **Mega Council** ratifies, scores every element 1–10, and for every element flagged for elevation: states **why**, gives **3 reasons**, and generates **10 solutions**.

Rating scale: **1–3** = replace, **4–6** = elevate, **7–8** = keep & sharpen, **9–10** = hero asset.

## 1. TIER 1 — INDIVIDUAL BOARD MEMBER REVIEWS

### The Conversion Copywriter
The headline "Build like Linear. Ship like Vercel." is a *developer* promise. Our buyer is a founder watching revenue plateau. They don't care about Vercel. They care that their growth is a flat line while a competitor's is a rocket. Lead with the curve, not the stack.

### The Direct-Response Strategist
There is no tension, no enemy, no stakes. Great DR names the villain (additive, incremental, "needle-moving" marketing) and contrasts it with the hero (exponential). The page must make "additive" feel like a slow death.

### The Brand Semiotician
"metaphysical" cannot appear as an explainer. The power is in the *unsaid*. Keep the wordmark, the violet, the faint cosmic shimmer — let the brand *feel* metaphysical without ever lecturing. Mystery is an asset class; don't spend it.

### The Behavioral Economist
Humans cannot intuit exponentials (the "exponential growth bias"). That's our wedge. Show two numbers side by side and let the gap do the persuading. A live, draggable proof beats any sentence.

### The Performance/Paid-Acquisition Lead
Every paid click must hit a single, legible promise in <3 seconds: *exponential, and you'll see the day it happens.* No six-card feature grids. One spine, one CTA, repeated.

### The Motion Designer
The old motion was tasteful but decorative. Motion here must *argue*. The exponential curve should literally draw itself and overtake the flat line — the animation is the proof, not garnish.

### The Web-App Engineer
"We build web apps, not websites" is told, not shown, everywhere on the competitor set. We can *prove* it by making this page a working app: an interactive growth simulator the visitor drives. The medium becomes the message.

### The Trust/Proof Officer
Bold claims (exponential, undeniable) without scaffolding read as hype. We need proof texture — the inflection marker, the "you'll know the exact day" guarantee, restrained metric callouts — to convert boldness into credibility.

## 2. TIER 2 — SEGMENT GUILD CONSENSUS

### 🛰️ Distro Guild (paid, organic, lifecycle, partnerships)
**Consensus:** One promise, one curve, one CTA. The page must survive being screenshotted — the hero + the curve alone should sell. Repeat the CTA at hero, after the proof, and at the mystery close. Kill "View source."

### 🎨 Design Gods (art direction, type, motion, color)
**Consensus:** Premium dark, cosmic-restrained. Violet→blue accent already in tokens — lean in. The exponential curve is the page's signature image; everything else recedes around it. Motion must be earned and must *mean* something. WCAG AA floor stays.

### 📈 Growth Board (offer, positioning, pricing psychology)
**Consensus:** Sell the *inflection*, not the service. Frame the buyer's status quo (additive) as the risk. The mystery is the qualifier — it pre-sorts serious buyers. CTA is an *application* ("Request the audit"), not a generic "Get started," to raise perceived value.

### ⚙️ Engineering Guild (perf, a11y, correctness)
**Consensus:** Stay inside the existing stack. SSR-safe effects only (AuroraGradient — pure CSS, 2KB). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for the curve draw and simulator. No new heavy WebGL deps. Interactivity must be keyboard-accessible.

## 5. STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING OF THE PAGE (information architecture)

The Council ratified this scroll spine — each section does exactly one job and hands off to the next:

1. **Nav** — wordmark + single CTA. (Identity + door.)
2. **Hero** — the exponential promise + the undefined word. (Hook in 3s.)
3. **Proof — The Curve** — animated additive vs exponential, with the inflection marked. (Make the claim undeniable.)
4. **Clarity pillars** — *When · Why · How much.* (Kill the "marketing is fuzzy" objection.)
5. **Live Growth Simulator** — visitor-driven; *is itself* the "we build web apps" proof. (Felt exponential + medium-as-message.)
6. **The Machine** — "We don't redesign websites. We build the machine." (The real offer.)
7. **The Mystery + CTA** — refuse to define; point to the door. (Qualify + convert.)
8. **Footer** — minimal, on-brand. (Exit without leaks.)

**Why this order converts:** promise → proof → objection-kill → felt-experience → offer → qualified ask. The interactive simulator is deliberately placed *after* belief is built and *before* the ask, so the visitor's own hands deliver the "exponential" realization right before the CTA.
