**Method:** Same teardown-and-qualify playbook used for the Pure Silk rebuild. Six prospects, six parallel deep-research agents, one identical rubric per agent (identity & footprint → full website teardown → reputation → marketing maturity → business-size/budget → 3-axis scoring). Same niche, same market as Pure Silk.

> **Data caveat (applies to all six):** Every prospect's live site and most aggregators (Yelp, Healthgrades, BuiltWith, Meta Ad Library) hard-block automated fetching (HTTP 403 / WAF). Teardowns are reconstructed from search-indexed page content, URL/permalink taxonomy, platform fingerprints, and third-party directories — reliable for platform, structure, SEO, reputation, and ownership; **less reliable for pixel-level visual polish and live page-speed.** Each prospect carries a 5-minute manual-verification checklist before outreach.

## Scoring scales

- **Site Badness** — 1 = immaculate · 10 = atrocious (*how bad the current site is*)
- **Elevation Upside** — 1 = no headroom · 10 = we can dramatically elevate it, easily (*how high we can lift them*)
- **Close Likelihood** — 1 = very unlikely · 10 = very likely to say yes (*will they hire us*)

## Cross-market patterns (reusable, à la Pure Silk)

Five of six share the **same structural weaknesses** — this is the repeatable CHITTA wedge in the Columbus aesthetics market:

1. **Templated, interchangeable design.** Every site except Rixis is a recognizable vendor/WordPress template. None reads as a $10K bespoke luxury brand. The in-person experience (4.8–5.0★ across the board) wildly outclasses the web presence.
2. **Booking is always a third-party redirect** — Vagaro (Blue Sky), Fresha (Luxe), Pabau (Medela), WellnessLiving (Beyond Skin), Zocdoc (Encore), multi-step request form (Rixis). **Native embedded booking is the universal, demonstrable conversion win.**
3. **Missing before/after galleries** — the single highest-converting asset in aesthetics — at Blue Sky, Rixis, Encore (and weak elsewhere).
4. **No financing surfaced** at Blue Sky / Rixis / Encore (table stakes; Medela & Luxe do it well — use them as the "your competitor already does this" proof).
5. **Fragmented brand** (Blue Sky's two domains, Beyond Skin's Shopify-shop split, Encore's `.com`/`.net`/subdomain sprawl) — consolidation is an easy, visible win.

**Standard pitch frame for all five:** *"Your reputation is a [4.9]; your website is a template."* Preserve their hard-won SEO + reviews, replace the templated shell and the third-party booking redirect with a bespoke, conversion-optimized $10K build, lead with a before/after gallery.

## Per-prospect verification checklist (5 min each, before outreach)

For every prospect, do a manual browser pass to lock the visual critique the 403s blocked:
- Load homepage on **desktop + mobile**; screenshot for the pitch.
- Run **Google PageSpeed Insights** on the domain.
- Click the **booking flow** end-to-end; confirm the third-party handoff.
- Check for a **before/after gallery** and **financing**.
- Confirm **Google Business Profile** star count and **Meta Ad Library** activity (paid-ad confirmation couldn't be retrieved by the agents).
- Blue Sky: screenshot the **two-domain split**. Encore: screenshot the **exposed `*.squarespace.com` subdomain + dead `.net`**. Beyond Skin: confirm which **Instagram handle** is primary.
