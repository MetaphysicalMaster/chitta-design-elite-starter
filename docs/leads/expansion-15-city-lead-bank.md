## CROSS-MARKET PATTERN (the repeatable wedge — same as Columbus)

The "worst website" almost always = one of five signatures, all of which sell themselves in a pitch:
1. **Exposed staging / leftover dev artifacts** — Avail (`*.elementor.cloud` indexed), Karma (`testkc.com`), Eternity (free `*.wordpress.com`), Gateway (`/default-homepage/`), Happy Clinic (duplicate `/homecopy`). *Show them the stranded URL = instant credibility.*
2. **Wrong-tool platforms** — Shopify e-comm theme as a med-spa site (SimplySkin), Joomla/K2 from 2006 (All About Me), `.info` domain + Squarespace (Medica), flat `.html`/`.htm` builders (Beautox, Karma).
3. **EMR/medical-marketing vendor templates** — Tebra/PatientPop/Officite clones (Timeless, Hanami, Darst, Dermaclinique, MidAmerica, Glo de Vie) — identical across thousands of practices; "you're renting a template a thousand others use."
4. **Booking offloaded off-brand** — Vagaro/Fresha/Square/Zocdoc/SimplyBook redirects everywhere; "you're renting your booking funnel."
5. **Reputation ≫ web presence** — every Tier-1 lead has strong reviews/credentials the site fails to monetize. Standard frame: *"Your reputation is a [4.9]; your website is a template."*
