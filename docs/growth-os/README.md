## Positioning guardrails (non-negotiable)
- **Register:** MetaMarketer editorial-luxury (Cormorant, black / antique-gold / off-white). NEVER CHITTA cyberpunk-HUD. NEVER JP's vocabulary ("AI employees", "lazy system", "leaky bucket", "fill your chairs" as a gimmick).
- **Transformation, not conversion:** we don't "add AI staff / replace headcount." We make *relationships the practice already earned* finally get answered. The patient who already chose them, invited back.
- **One thought per interaction:** sell the *department*, not five tools. Lead with ONE wedge (missed-call OR reactivation), prove it, then expand.
- **DistroGod owns every patient-facing word**, conditioned on each clinic's injector voice profile. These docs are a structural draft — run a **DistroGod voice-pass before any client send.**

## Compliance do-not-say list (FTC / TCPA / HIPAA — also non-negotiable)
- **Never** incentivize a *positive* review or gate a reward on the star rating. Raffle the **act of giving feedback** only (FTC).
- **Never** put borrowed numbers (JP's +400% / 92% / etc.) as *our* results. Cite the underlying **research as industry mechanism**, never as a forecast of the client's outcome.
- **Never** name tools/models/credits on client-facing surfaces (tools invisible — we sell craft + outcome).
- **Never** auto-text a list without documented prior express written consent. **Never** auto-publish before/after patient photos (needs separate signed consent).
- Reactivation messages are **treatment-agnostic + PHI-free** ("we'd love to see you back" — never "time for your Botox"). The AI books/confirms/routes; **never** gives medical advice or quotes clinical outcomes; anything clinical → human callback.
- **Pre-sale gates baked into setup:** signed BAA · TCPA consent provenance · A2P 10DLC registration started Day 1 (2–3 wk lead time).
