## The core move (why this wins)
Everyone cold-pitches a *promise*. You open with a **finished gift**: *"I rebuilt
your website — here's the live link."* That's an un-ignorable pattern-interrupt — the
work is already done, on their own brand, before they've paid a cent. The live site
(door) carries the on-site **concierge demo** (wedge) and a link to their personalized
**/offer** page (proof + packages). One gift, one link, one conversation.

## The asset chain (what you send → where it goes)
1. **Outreach message** (email / IG DM / LinkedIn) → contains their **live rebuilt site URL** + their **offer code**.
2. **Live site** → they see their new front door + the AI concierge that books.
3. **themetamarketer.com/offer** → they enter their code → personalized proposal: the mirror read of their *current* site, the BEFORE→NOW contrasts, the new-site preview, the Growth-OS packages, one door.
4. **/start** (the one CTA) → book the free Front-Door / Reactivation Audit.
5. **Audit call** → leak numbers from *their* list → founder offer → close.
> The /offer machine **tracks `viewedAt`** — you'll know the moment they open it. Time your follow-up to that.

## Channels — ranked, multi-touch the SAME lead
Med-spas live on Instagram and answer email; owners are on LinkedIn; the warmest convert by text.
1. **Email** to the owner/practice (primary — carries the link + code cleanly).
2. **Instagram DM** (med-spas are IG-native; often fastest reply).
3. **LinkedIn** to the owner (B2B credibility).
4. **Phone / text** (warmest leads only, after a reply).
Run 2–3 channels per lead; stop the sequence the instant they reply.

## Cadence — 4 touches over ~9 days (per lead)
- **Day 0 — the gift.** Personalized Loom (90s) + email/DM: *"rebuilt your site, here's the link + your private proposal code."*
- **Day 2 — the leak.** If `viewedAt` but no reply → the one-page leak read (their missed-call + dormant-patient estimate, framed as research-based ranges, never invented). If not viewed → resend the link, one line.
- **Day 5 — a second insight.** A specific, useful observation (a competitor out-ranking them on reviews; a treatment they under-feature) — value, not a nudge.
- **Day 9 — the break-up.** *"I'll leave the site up for you either way — here if you ever want the engine behind it."* (Soft close; break-ups get replies.)
Stop on reply. Founder rate offered live on the call, to ONE lead, for a named case study.

## The 90-second Loom (the highest-converting single asset)
1. Their **current** site on screen (2 sec — the contrast).
2. Cut to their **rebuilt** live site — scroll the hero, the real before/afters, the reviews.
3. **Open the concierge** — let it book a sample appointment ("this catches the calls your front desk can't").
4. One line: *"I also found [N] in your existing patient list going uncontacted — that's in your proposal. Code's in the email. 15 minutes?"*
No deck. The screen is the pitch.

## Targeting — warm first, then the cascade
- **Now:** the 7 rebuilt (each has a live site + code). Highest intent.
