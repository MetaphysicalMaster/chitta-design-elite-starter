Medical Dermatology

Clinical care, academically grounded.

The full breadth of medical and surgical dermatology — delivered with the precision you'd expect from a teaching faculty.

  • Skin Cancer Care

    Full-body screening, biopsy and surgical excision with academic rigor.

  • Acne

    Personalized medical regimens for teens and adults — including resistant cases.

  • Eczema & Dermatitis

    Targeted relief and long-term management of inflammatory skin disease.

  • Psoriasis

    Advanced topical, light and biologic therapies tailored to severity.

  • Rosacea

    Calming chronic redness and flares with evidence-based protocols.

  • Surgical Dermatology

    In-office removal of cysts, lesions and suspicious growths.

  • Mole & Lesion Checks

    Dermoscopic evaluation and mapping for early detection.

  • Pediatric Dermatology

    Gentle, specialized care for the youngest patients.

The Spa at Encore

Aesthetics, elevated.

A physician-supervised med-spa where dermatologic science meets luxury. The same expertise behind your medical care, now devoted to how you look and feel.

  • Botox & Dysport

    Expert neuromodulator artistry for a natural, refreshed look.

  • Dermal Fillers

    Restore volume and contour with precise, conservative technique.

  • Sciton Halo Laser

    Hybrid fractional resurfacing for tone, texture and glow.

  • IPL Photofacial

    Erase sun damage, redness and brown spots with intense pulsed light.

  • CoolSculpting

    Non-surgical fat reduction that freezes away stubborn pockets.

  • RF Microneedling

    Radiofrequency collagen remodeling for firmer, smoother skin.

  • Medical Facials & Peels

    Clinical-grade facials, chemical peels and dermaplaning.

  • Skincare & Memberships
